AgentiCCells

AgentiCCells

Small teams of AI agents that do one job for you. Official site: agenticcells.ai. Checked 20 November 2026.

This design prints on A4 as a one-page brief with its sources.

In one line

“AgentiCCells builds small teams of AI agents, called cells, that do one job for you in your own accounts, with a page where you can watch them and stop them.” (AgentiCCells, about itself)1

What it is

A cell is a small team of AI agents with one job. It runs in your environment, meaning your own accounts and repositories, with a conductor agent that hands out work and keeps the record, and keys that you hold to steer it and take it over.1

The first offer is the website cell: an agent team builds a public static website from your photos, videos and description, and hands it over in a GitHub repository you own.2

What it sells is "the setup, the guidance and the trust"; the software itself is free and open. The name is always plural, and the "CC" stands for Creative Commons.3 4

Status, as the company states it: "No cell of any size has been sold." Every larger price is labelled a first proposal.5 6

Who it is for

  • "Teams that want AI agents doing real work, inside their own accounts and repositories, and want to see and stop what those agents do."1
  • For the website cell: artists, musicians, AI people and technologists, consultants and companies.2
  • Partners: freelancers, small consultancies and larger firms who bring customers.12
  • Not for anyone who wants a hosted service that keeps their data.1

How it works

  1. A brief per agentEach agent gets one job, one name and its own key pair; only the public half is published.14
  2. A board of filesWork is a folder of markdown files; agents talk through files, commit messages and encrypted sgit vault lanes.14
  3. Gated releasesNothing ships without the checks: a validator, a leak scan and a wording check.14 13
  4. A stop fileAnyone can halt the team with board/HOLD.md, and everything is logged.14 13
  5. Hand it overThe work ends in your repository, with its full history, to run on any static host.2 15

Products and parts

  • Website cell

    A static site from your photos, videos and description, in your own GitHub. The page shows a £250 launch price, usually £500, which it says may change; nothing has been sold yet.2

  • Cell sizes

    One agent, a few sessions, a team, or a network of conductors: £500, £1,500, £5,000 and £20,000, all labelled first proposal.6

  • The console

    A read-only view of the board, agents, keys and activity, built for its own team.16

  • The hub

    Lists every cell made: "We list; we do not sell."17

  • Partner programme

    Freelancers, consultancies and firms sell; the sale is split 50/50. No partner has signed yet.12
