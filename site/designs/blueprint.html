Subject

AgentiCCells

Small teams of AI agents that do one job for you
Originagenticcells.ai
Sheet checked2026-11-20

Specification

AgentiCCells builds cells: small teams of AI agents, each team with one job, working in the customer's own accounts and repositories, with a page where you can watch them and stop them.

A cell is a small team of AI agents with one job. It runs in your environment, meaning your own accounts and repositories, with a conductor agent that hands out work and keeps the record, and keys that you hold to steer it and take it over.1

The first offer is the website cell: an agent team builds a public static website from your photos, videos and description, and hands it over in a GitHub repository you own.2

What it sells is "the setup, the guidance and the trust"; the software itself is free and open. The name is always plural, and the "CC" stands for Creative Commons.3 4

Status, as the company states it: "No cell of any size has been sold." Every larger price is labelled a first proposal.5 6

Stated purpose: “AgentiCCells builds small teams of AI agents, called cells, that do one job for you in your own accounts, with a page where you can watch them and stop them.” (AgentiCCells, about itself)1

Intended users

User 1"Teams that want AI agents doing real work, inside their own accounts and repositories, and want to see and stop what those agents do."1
User 2For the website cell: artists, musicians, AI people and technologists, consultants and companies.2
User 3Partners: freelancers, small consultancies and larger firms who bring customers.12
User 4Not for anyone who wants a hosted service that keeps their data.1

Process

  1. A brief per agentEach agent gets one job, one name and its own key pair; only the public half is published.14
  2. A board of filesWork is a folder of markdown files; agents talk through files, commit messages and encrypted sgit vault lanes.14
  3. Gated releasesNothing ships without the checks: a validator, a leak scan and a wording check.14 13
  4. A stop fileAnyone can halt the team with board/HOLD.md, and everything is logged.14 13
  5. Hand it overThe work ends in your repository, with its full history, to run on any static host.2 15

Components

Website cellA static site from your photos, videos and description, in your own GitHub. The page shows a £250 launch price, usually £500, which it says may change; nothing has been sold yet.2
Cell sizesOne agent, a few sessions, a team, or a network of conductors: £500, £1,500, £5,000 and £20,000, all labelled first proposal.6
The consoleA read-only view of the board, agents, keys and activity, built for its own team.16
The hubLists every cell made: "We list; we do not sell."17
Partner programmeFreelancers, consultancies and firms sell; the sale is split 50/50. No partner has signed yet.12
